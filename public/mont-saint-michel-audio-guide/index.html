What the guide covers

The route starts at the town gate and climbs the island the way it was built to be climbed: up the Grande Rue, past the parish church of Saint-Pierre, to the terrace at the foot of the abbey, then back down along the ramparts with the widest views over the bay. Along the way the stories explain what you're looking at and why it is there:

  • Bishop Aubert, the archangel Michael and the founding of the sanctuary in 708.
  • How medieval builders raised an abbey and La Merveille on a granite rock ringed by quicksand and racing tides.
  • The pilgrims who crossed the bay on foot, and the dangers they faced.
  • The fortress that held out through the Hundred Years' War.
  • The decades when the holy mount was a prison.
  • The tides, and how the bay works.

The stories are written by local historians and expert guides, and you start each one yourself at the spot — nothing plays automatically, so you can stop, look around and move on when you're ready. For how the island is laid out, see our map and walking route.

How it works

  1. Buy the guide. One payment, instant access, no subscription.
  2. Download it at home. Install the TouringBee app on iOS or Android and download the tour over Wi-Fi before you go.
  3. Open the map at the gate. GPS shows where you are on the offline map, so you don't need a signal on the island.
  4. Start each story at its stop. Pause, take a break, pick up where you left off.

Self-guided with an audio guide, or just on your own?

You can walk the island without any guide — the village and ramparts are free. Most visitors do exactly that and simply follow the crowd up the Grande Rue and back down the same way. The audio guide adds two things: a route that leaves the main street where it's worth it, and the stories behind what you see. If you are short on time, it also helps you avoid doubling back.

Is there a free audio guide for Mont-Saint-Michel?

There's no free full audio guide from us; the full tour is a one-off purchase from €9.99. You can listen to the free sample above before you decide. The village and ramparts are free to walk, and the abbey's own audio guide is a paid add-on to the abbey ticket.

Frequently asked questions

Does the audio guide include the abbey ticket?

No. The guide covers the village and the ramparts up to the abbey. Entry to the abbey is a separate ticket.

How long is the tour?

The route is about 2 hours of walking with 17 stops. Allow more time for the whole island, especially if you also visit the abbey.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Download the tour before you go; the map, GPS and audio then work without mobile data.

Which languages are available?

Eight: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish and Russian.

What do I need to use it?

A smartphone with iOS or Android and the free TouringBee app. Headphones are more comfortable in the crowds.

How long do I have access?

One payment gives you access for a year, with no subscription.

Do the stories start automatically?

No — you start each story yourself when you reach the stop, so you set the pace.
